L’argent attend. Le colis arrive.

Money waits. The parcel arrives. That one promise is the whole brand: BayamSafe holds the buyer’s money until the parcel is in their hands, so sellers and buyers who have never met can trade with confidence.

Who we are

Built for the bayam-sellam

Bayam-sellam are the traders who buy here and sell there; they keep Cameroon’s markets moving. Today they sell on WhatsApp, TikTok and Facebook, and every sale starts with the same doubt: will the money come, will the parcel come? We exist to remove that doubt. Everything we make should feel like a firm handshake at Mokolo market: solid, warm, plain-spoken, proud.

SolideSolid

Money is serious. We never joke about it, never blur numbers, always say exactly where the funds are.

ChaleureuxWarm

We speak like a trusted cousin in the trade, not a bank. Short sentences, real words, a little smile.

Made in Cameroon for Cameroon. Our references are ours: Fako, the market basket, the morning sun over Buea.

The mark

Star Lock

A padlock whose keyhole is the gold star of the Cameroonian flag. It explains escrow without a word: the money is locked, and only the right key opens it. The star says whose lock it is. It is the simplest mark we could make, so it stays readable from a shop sign down to a 16 px browser tab.

Construction

The body is 120 × 94 with a 24 corner radius. The shackle is a half circle of radius 36, drawn as thick as the star’s arms are long. The star sits on the body’s vertical axis, just above its centre, where a real keyhole would be.

Clear space & minimum size
24 px
16 px

Keep a quarter of the mark’s width clear on every side. The full mark holds down to 16 px. For one-colour printing (stamps, embroidery, rider vests), use the mono version with the star knocked out.

Using the mark

Four rules

Do

Use the full colour mark on sand or white, and the reversed lockup on night forest.

Don’t

Rotate or tilt it. A tilted lock looks forced open.

Don’t

Put it on gold, or recolour the star. The star is always gold, or knocked out in one-colour use.

Don’t

Stretch, squash, add shadows, gradients or outlines.

Signature element

La Ligne de Crête

Every BayamSafe journey is a climb up Mount Cameroon. We draw order progress as the mountain’s ridge: each step is a waypoint, and the summit, marked by the gold star from our lock, is the moment the lock opens and the seller is paid. It turns a status bar into a story our users already know.

  1. Lien envoyéLink sent
  2. Argent bloquéMoney locked
  3. Livreur en routeRider on the way
  4. Colis récupéréParcel picked up
  5. Code donnéCode given
  6. Vendeur payéSeller paid

On the checkout page it replaces the status badge. In the bot, a short ridge sticker marks each step. On the landing page it draws itself as you scroll.

The line is always gold up to the current step, then dotted. Waypoints are rings; the summit is a filled sun. Never draw it going downhill.

Voice

How we sound

French first, English always available. Plain words a market trader uses. Say where the money is in every message about money.

We say
“Votre argent est bloqué chez BayamSafe. Le vendeur ne le reçoit qu’après votre code.”
Your money is held by BayamSafe. The seller only gets it after your code.
Not
“Transaction escrow initiée avec succès via notre infrastructure sécurisée.”
Jargon. Nobody at the market talks like this.
We say
“Code incorrect. Il vous reste 2 essais.”
Wrong code. You have 2 tries left.
Not
“Oups ! 😅 Une erreur est survenue…”
Vague, and jokey about money.
